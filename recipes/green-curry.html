Simple Green Curry

Description

Simple green curry is a quick last minute meal for the family.

  • 2 cups white rice
  • Chicken breast 500g
  • 1 Onion
  • Premade sauce of choice
  1. Dice onion and chicken to desired size chunks
  2. Add 2 cups white rice to microwave safe bowl with 3.5 cups water
  3. Heat a pan/pot and cook chicken through before adding onion
  4. Microwave rice in 5 min intervals till desired texture(do this in the backround)
  5. Cook onion till translucent
  6. Add premade sauce to pan and allow to simmer till warm
  7. Add desired amount of rice to bowl(s) and top with curry
