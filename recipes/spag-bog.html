Spaghetti Bog

Description

This delightful italian dish is a super simple staple meal. cooked in minutes it fast, easy and delicious.

  • Spaghetti pasta
  • Mince meat 500g
  • 1 Onion
  • Premade sauce of choice
  • Parmisan cheese
  1. Dice onion to desired size chunks
  2. Boil pot of water and add pasta stir reguarly to prevent pasta sticking
  3. Heat a pan and brown mince before adding onion
  4. Cook onion till translucent
  5. Add premade sauce to pan and allow to simmer till warm
  6. Strain pasta and add desired amount to bowl(s)
  7. Add disred amount of meat to bowl(s) and top with parmisan cheese
