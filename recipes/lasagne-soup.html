Lasagne Soup

Description

This delightful aussie twist on the italian dish is a super simple staple meal. cooked in minutes it fast, easy and delicious.

  • Pasta sheets
  • Mince meat 1kg
  • 1 Onion
  • Premade sauce of choice
  • Shredded cheese
  • Cooking cream 300ml
  1. Dice onion to desired size chunks
  2. Heat a pot and brown mince before adding onion
  3. Cook onion till translucent
  4. Add premade sauce and cooking cream to pot and allow to simmer till warm
  5. Snap pasta sheets into smaller pieces inside the pot
  6. Add desired amount of cheese (suggest 1 cup) to pot
  7. Add disred amount of mix to bowl(s)
